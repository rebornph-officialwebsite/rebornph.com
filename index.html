📜 Privacy Policy (REBORN‑PH Platform)

Introduction

REBORN‑PH values your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our freelance platform. By accessing our services, you agree to the practices described here.

Information We Collect

  • Personal details such as name, email, and contact information when you register.
  • Professional information including skills, portfolio, and work history.
  • Transactional data related to projects, payments, and communications.
  • Technical data such as IP address, browser type, and usage patterns.

How We Use Your Information

  • To connect freelancers with clients and facilitate project collaboration.
  • To process payments securely and maintain transaction records.
  • To improve platform functionality and user experience.
  • To communicate updates, opportunities, and support services.

Data Protection

We implement strict security measures to protect your information. Your data is stored securely and only accessible to authorized personnel. We do not sell or share your personal information with third parties except as required by law or to fulfill contractual obligations.

User Rights

You have the right to access, update, or delete your personal information. You may also request limitations on how your data is processed. For inquiries, contact us at contact@reborn.com.
